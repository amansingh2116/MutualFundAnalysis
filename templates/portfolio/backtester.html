    ⚠️ Things to keep in mind about this backtest ▼
    • Transaction Costs — Real investments incur stamp duty (0.005% on purchase), AMC expense ratios (0.1%–1.5% p.a. embedded in NAV), and brokerage/platform fees. This simulator ignores these — actual returns will be slightly lower.
    • Exit Load — Most equity mutual funds charge 1% exit load if redeemed within 1 year of each purchase (FIFO basis). Debt funds vary. This simulator does not deduct exit load — factor this in if you plan frequent redemptions.
    • Taxes — Equity funds: STCG (held <1 year) taxed at 20%; LTCG (held ≥1 year) taxed at 12.5% with ₹1,25,000/year exemption. Debt/liquid funds: gains taxed at your income slab. High-frequency switching strategies may significantly erode returns after tax.
    • Perfect execution assumed — triggers fire exactly at threshold with next available NAV. Real-world execution may have delay and slippage.
    • Survivorship bias — only currently existing funds appear in search. Funds that closed or merged are excluded.
    • Not investment advice. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

    ⚠️ Disclaimer & Assumptions
    • Tax is estimated using user-entered rates and FIFO cost-basis from the transaction ledger. Rules change over time; update the rates before analysis when tax accuracy matters.
    • Actual tax may differ because of exemptions, surcharge, cess, indexation history, grandfathering, set-offs, and personal circumstances.
    • Unrealised gains at portfolio end date are not taxed - only realised redemptions are included.
    • Inflation adjustment uses the Fisher equation: Real XIRR roughly equals (1+Nominal)/(1+Inflation) - 1.
    • All figures are indicative. Consult a tax advisor before making financial decisions.

    ℹ️ Monte Carlo Methodology
    • Simulations use Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) parameterised by the strategy's own historical μ (mean log return) and σ (volatility) from the backtest period.
    • Each path starts from the portfolio's ending value and projects forward for the chosen horizon, adding monthly SIP if configured.
    • Results are NOT predictions. Past volatility does not guarantee future volatility.
    • Seed is fixed for reproducibility — same inputs always produce the same fan chart.
