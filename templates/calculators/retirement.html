💡 How the 25x Retirement Rule Works & Key Guidelines
🔥 The 25x Rule (4% Safe Withdrawal Rate)
The 25x rule states that your retirement corpus should equal 25 times your future annual expenses at retirement:
  • Withdraw 4% of corpus in Year 1 of retirement to cover living expenses.
  • Increase withdrawal by inflation annually. Historically, this corpus lasts 30+ years without running out.
🛡️ Post-Retirement Bucket Strategy
Structure your corpus into 3 risk-adjusted buckets upon retirement:
  • Bucket 1 (1–3 Years): Liquid & Ultra Short Debt Funds for immediate monthly SWP payouts.
  • Bucket 2 (4–7 Years): Short Duration & Corporate Bond funds generating steady income.
  • Bucket 3 (8+ Years): Equity Funds to outpace long-term inflation.
📢 Note: Revisit your retirement plan every 3–5 years as your income, expenses, and lifestyle evolve.
