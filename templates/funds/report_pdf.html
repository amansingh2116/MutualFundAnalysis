{{ narratives.risk_text }}

Jensen's Alpha (+/-)
Measures the excess return generated by the fund manager above CAPM risk-adjusted expectations. Positive Alpha proves active stock picking skill.
Sharpe & Sortino Ratios
Sharpe measures return per unit of total risk. Sortino focuses specifically on downside volatility. Higher ratios indicate superior risk efficiency.
Beta (< 1.0 or > 1.0)
Beta measures sensitivity to market index movements. Beta < 1.0 is defensive with lower volatility; Beta > 1.0 amplifies market rallies and corrections.
Maximum Drawdown
Captures the worst peak-to-trough decline experienced by an investor. Lower drawdown indicates stronger capital preservation during market crashes.
