Scoring Framework

This scorecard uses a category-normalized, multi-factor model (v2) to evaluate funds across 6 independent pillars. Each pillar is scored 0–100 and combined using category-adaptive weights:

  • Performance (30%) — Trailing CAGR (1Y/3Y/5Y), rolling win rates (3Y & 5Y vs 0%), excess return vs benchmark, return consistency (calendar year std dev).
  • Risk / Stability (25%) — Sortino ratio, max drawdown, downside capture, Sharpe ratio, Information Ratio, Beta, Up/Down asymmetry. Emphasises downside control over total volatility.
  • Cost (15%) — Expense ratio vs category-type norms (direct vs regular, equity/debt/index/hybrid), AUM scale factor.
  • Composition & Liquidity (15%, equity/hybrid only) — Top-10 concentration, total holdings count, sector HHI, portfolio liquidity (large/mid/small-cap weightings), portfolio turnover.
  • Debt Quality (15%, debt funds; 5% hybrid) — Credit rating profile, modified duration mismatch, yield-to-maturity vs category, % sovereign/AAA holdings.
  • Manager Quality (15%) — Manager tenure, alpha track record, AMC governance score, number of funds managed, closet indexing check (R²).
  • Red Flag Penalty (up to −20 pts) — Structural risks deducted from composite: missing benchmark, very high ER, low AUM, extreme concentration, SEBI regulatory action, high turnover without IR justification.

Weight table differs for Index/ETF (higher Cost weight, no Manager/Composition) and Debt funds (no Composition, higher Cost). Skipped pillars are excluded and weights redistributed.

Normalization & Fallbacks

Each metric is linearly normalized to [0, 100] using fixed thresholds calibrated for the Indian mutual fund market. When data is missing for a pillar, the score for that pillar is excluded and weights are redistributed across available pillars. The confidence level (Rated / Provisional / Unrated) reflects data coverage.

Category Rank

💡 How Monte Carlo Simulation Works & How to Interpret
⚙️ Simulation Methodology
Monte Carlo simulation projects future NAV paths without assuming a rigid normal distribution:
  • Historical Bootstrap Resampling: Randomly samples from actual daily historical returns, preserving fat tails, skewness, and market crash jumps.
  • 500 Simulated Trajectories: Generates 500 parallel future NAV paths over 1-Year (252D), 3-Year (756D), or 5-Year (1260D) horizons.
  • Percentile Distribution Analysis: Sorts simulated NAVs at each future day to compute the 95th, 75th, 50th (median), 25th, and 5th percentile bands.
🎯 Percentile Fan & Goal Planning Guide
95th Percentile 🟢 : Bull Case Scenario — Top 5% optimistic outcome during strong bull market rallies.
75th Percentile 🔵 : Above-Average Path — Favourable growth trajectory assuming positive market conditions.
50th Percentile ⚪ : Median Expected NAV — Central 50/50 baseline expectation for financial goal calculations.
25th Percentile 🟧 : Below-Average Path — Mild correction scenario. Good benchmark for conservative SIP planning.
5th Percentile 🔴 : Bear Case Scenario — Bottom 5% severe crash outcome. Essential for stress-testing emergency capital.
💡 Actionable Tip: For critical financial goals (e.g., child education or home down payment), ensure your required target corpus is met by at least the 25th percentile path, not just the 50th median.

💡 How Quantitative Forecasting & Backtesting Work & How to Interpret
🔮 Three Target Forecasting Modes
  • 📈 Return Forecast (Fan Chart): Projects absolute NAV level and 68%/80%/95% confidence bands using 🏆 Ensemble, Holt, ARIMA, XGBoost/RF, and LSTM deep net models over 30D–180D horizons.
  • 🎯 Direction Forecast (% Bullish): Classifies future NAV direction (Bullish 📈 vs Bearish 📉) using Logistic Regression, XGBoost, SVM, and Random Forest classifiers.
  • 🌡️ Volatility Forecast (Risk Regime): Models future price dispersion (% p.a.) using GARCH(1,1), EGARCH (asymmetric leverage), RiskMetrics EWMA, and ML volatility models.
📊 Walk-Forward Backtesting Metrics
Every model is rigorously evaluated out-of-sample over the last 90 days:
MAPE ↓ (Mean Absolute % Error): Lower is better. Measures average forecast percentage error against actual historical NAV.
RMSE ↓ (Root Mean Square Error): Dollar/rupee deviation error penalising large outliers.
Dir. Hit Rate ↑: % of trading days the model correctly predicted direction (>55% indicates statistical edge).
🥇 Best Model Badge: Automatically highlights the model with lowest out-of-sample MAPE error over the last 90 days.
🏆 Pro Tip: Always pay closest attention to the 🏆 Ensemble Model. It blends all enabled models using inverse-MAPE weighting, providing the most reliable out-of-sample forecast precision.

💡 How the Quant Signal Engine Works & How to Interpret
⚙️ Mathematical Methodology
The Signal Engine processes daily NAV history through 5 weighted quantitative factor pillars:
  • Trend & Momentum (20%): SMA20/50/200 alignment + 1M/3M/6M price momentum.
  • Technical Oscillators (20%): RSI(14), MACD histogram, and Bollinger %B mean-reversion.
  • Forecast Model Consensus (25%): Ensemble, Holt, ARIMA, XGBoost, & Direction Classifiers (% Bullish).
  • Volatility & Tail Risk (20%): ATR volatility regime, GARCH(1,1) forecast, and 95% CVaR tail risk.
  • Return Consistency (15%): Fraction of positive monthly returns over past 12 months.
