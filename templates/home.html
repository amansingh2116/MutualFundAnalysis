AMFI Monthly Industry Flows Notice

AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) publishes official mutual fund category inflows and AUM figures on a monthly cycle (typically released around the 10th of each month). If the live automated sync is updating or AMFI source formatting is in transition, the latest official reports are accessible directly from AMFI.

🏛 Open AMFI Data Portal ↗
📍 Direct Step-by-Step Navigation on AMFI India:
  1. Go to the official AMFI website: www.amfiindia.com
  2. In the top menu, select Research & Information → AMFI Data.
  3. Click on Monthly Industry Data / Trend Reports.
  4. Choose your desired month & year to see full Inflows, Redemptions, and Net Assets across Equity, Debt, Hybrid, and Index/ETF categories.
