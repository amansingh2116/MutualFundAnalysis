How do you decide between index and active funds?
I've been comparing cost, category rank persistence, and downside behavior across rolling windows. Index consistently wins in large-cap, but mid/small-cap still shows alpha in select active funds. Would love to hear practical frameworks from other long-term investors.
mutual funds index funds active vs passive portfolio
PR
Priya R.
Look at rolling returns and category rank persistence, not just one-year performance. A fund that consistently ranks in Q1–Q2 over 7+ years is worth the expense ratio premium.
KM
Karan M.
For core allocation, index all the way. For satellite (10–20%), I compare fund manager track record across market cycles. Mid-cap active still has room to beat Nifty Midcap 150.
SN
Shruti N.
Also check for overlap. Many "diversified" active funds in large-cap hold 60%+ of Nifty 50 stocks anyway. The alpha claim barely holds after survivorship bias is corrected.

My SIP rebalancing rules — what works for me
After 8 years of tracking, here's what I do: 1) Rebalance annually, not more. 2) Only if equity allocation drifts >5% from target. 3) Never sell in a down year — just redirect fresh SIPs. 4) Smallcap % capped at 15% of total portfolio at all times. Happy to discuss tradeoffs.
SIP rebalancing portfolio management
RS
Rahul S.
The "no sell in a down year" rule is controversial but I've found it psychologically much easier. The portfolio usually recovers before you'd want to sell anyway.
MJ
Meera J.
Do you use goal-based buckets or a single combined view for rebalancing? I find that mixing goals makes rebalancing decisions ambiguous.
VT
Vivek T.
15% smallcap cap is conservative. At what age / stage did you set this and how does it adjust as you approach retirement?

PE ratio of Nifty 50 — are we expensive right now?
Current trailing PE is sitting at ~22.5x. Historical median is ~20x. That's not bubble territory but not cheap either. More importantly, forward earnings growth estimates are being revised upward. Sharing a chart of Nifty PE bands since 2010.
