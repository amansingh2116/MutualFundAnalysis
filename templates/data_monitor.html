ℹ️ How the Pipeline Works

🏛️
NAV Data Source
Daily NAVs are fetched from mfapi.in (AMFI mirror). Published by AMFI at ~8 PM IST, ingested at 2 AM IST daily. Incremental fetch — only new rows are added.
⚙️
Analytics Engine
After NAV ingestion, rolling returns (1Y/3Y/5Y), Sharpe, Sortino, Beta, Alpha, drawdowns are computed per fund. Skipped automatically on weekends when no new NAV arrives.
🔄
Weekly Refresh Cycle
~2,300 funds are split into 7 equal shards. Each shard runs on a different weekday (~330 funds, 4–5h). All funds are refreshed within 7 days. Metadata (AUM, ER, manager) refreshed every 7 days.
