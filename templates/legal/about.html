About MF Analysis

A free, independent platform for serious Indian mutual fund research.

🎯 What We Do

MF Analysis is a research platform built for Indian retail investors who want to go beyond star ratings. We aggregate NAV data, compute risk-adjusted metrics, build quartile rankings, and present everything in a clean, data-first interface — completely free of charge.

Our philosophy is simple: data over narratives. We believe every investor deserves access to institutional-quality fund research without a subscription fee or paywall.

📦 What We Cover

We intentionally focus on a subset of the AMFI universe — the plans that actually matter for long-term wealth creation:

🔓
Open-Ended Funds
Daily liquidity and NAV transparency. No locked tenures or thin exchange trading.
📈
Direct Growth Funds
No distributor commission. Growth option only. The highest-return variant of any fund.
🏦
ETFs
Exchange-traded funds listed on NSE/BSE. Passive, low-cost, and increasingly important in Indian portfolios.

Regular plans, IDCW/dividend variants, Close-Ended schemes, and Interval funds are excluded platform-wide. Read our blog → to understand the philosophy.

💸 Pricing

MF Analysis is completely free to use. No subscription, no premium tier, no ads. All research tools — screener, portfolio tracker, quartile rankings, calculators — are available to every visitor.

The platform is not open-source. The codebase, models, and data pipelines are proprietary.

📊 Data Sources

NAV data is sourced from AMFI and mfapi.in. Benchmark index data is sourced from NSE Direct API and Yahoo Finance as a fallback. Portfolio overlap and holdings data is sourced from Morningstar and fund house disclosures. All data is updated on Indian business days.

⚠️ Disclaimer

MF Analysis is a research and information platform only. Nothing on this site constitutes investment advice, portfolio management, or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Please consult a SEBI-registered investment advisor before making financial decisions.
