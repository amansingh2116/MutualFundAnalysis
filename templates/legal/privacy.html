Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your username and password (hashed). We do not require an email address. We store your in-app preferences (benchmark watchlist, market strip metrics) in our database so you can access them from any device when logged in.

Portfolio data you upload (CAS statements) is stored only for your active session and used solely to compute analytics you request.

2. What We Don't Collect

  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Payment information (the Platform is free)
  • Personal financial data beyond what you explicitly upload
  • Third-party tracking or advertising cookies

3. How We Use Your Data

Data we collect is used exclusively to provide and improve the Platform. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Sessions

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. No third-party analytics or advertising cookies are set. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us via the contact page.

6. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. We use HTTPS. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

We may update this policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
